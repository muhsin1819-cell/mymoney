MY MONEY - personal finance app for iPhone
==========================================

Files in this folder (upload ALL of them together):
  index.html, sw.js, manifest.webmanifest, icon-180.png, icon-192.png, icon-512.png

1. PUT IT ONLINE (free, one time, ~10 minutes, from a computer)
   - Go to github.com and create a free account.
   - Click "+" > "New repository". Name it e.g. mymoney. Choose Public. Create.
   - Click "uploading an existing file", drag in all 6 files, click "Commit changes".
   - Open Settings > Pages. Under "Branch" choose "main" and "/ (root)", click Save.
   - After 1-2 minutes your app is at:  https://YOUR-USERNAME.github.io/mymoney/
   Only the empty app code is public. Your financial data never leaves your iPhone.

2. INSTALL ON IPHONE
   - Open that link in Safari.
   - Tap Share > "Add to Home Screen" > Add.
   - ALWAYS open My Money from the Home Screen icon (Safari keeps separate data).
   - FIRST TIME: tap "Restore from a backup file instead", choose MyMoney-starting-data.json
     (save it to Files / iCloud Drive first) and enter the temporary passcode you were given.
     Then go to Settings > Change passcode and set your own.
   - Then follow "Set up your starting point" (bank balances, exchange rate, SIP funds).

3. KEEP IT SAFE
   - Settings > Export encrypted backup > Save to Files > iCloud Drive. Do this every couple of weeks.
   - If you forget the passcode, the data cannot be recovered - not by anyone.
   - New phone: install as above, then "Restore from a backup file instead".

4. UPDATING THE APP LATER
   - Replace the files on GitHub with the new ones. Your data stays on the phone and is not affected.
